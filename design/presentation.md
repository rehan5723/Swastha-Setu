# SwasthaSetu — Login / Registration Presentation

Overview
- Two high-resolution screens (login + registration) in `design/` as SVG files.
- Split-screen layout: left 60% form in white card; right 40% high-end glowing tech art.

Key UI decisions
- Prominent brand mark with stylized bridge and integrated medical cross to signal trust and India-focused identity.
- `#0F52BA` primary CTA to convey trust, authority and clarity.
- Left side uses minimal, clinical whitespace for focus; right side uses depth and glow to show AI-powered diagnostics.

How to use
- Open `design/login-mockup.svg` and `design/register-mockup.svg` in any vector editor (Figma/Illustrator) to refine.
- Export at higher DPI from vector editor for marketing assets.
